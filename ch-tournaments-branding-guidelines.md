# CH Tournaments — Branding Guidelines

*A reference doc for CH Tournaments' visual identity. Warm, confident, and built for the community — not corporate, not generic.*

---

## The "Don't" List

These are the traps golf communities fall into. Avoid all of them.

**Don't look like a country club.**
No crests, no coat of arms, no crossed-clubs logos, no script/cursive typefaces, no dark green and gold "traditional" palettes. That's the thing you're the antidote to.

**Don't use cliché golf imagery.**
No stock photos of a guy in a tucked-in polo mid-backswing. No aerial drone shots of fairways as hero banners. If you use photography, it should be *your people at your events* — candid, real, slightly chaotic. Authentic community moments.

**Don't over-design.**
Tons of whitespace. Minimal navigation. No gradients layered on gradients. Restraint signals confidence.

**Don't use more than 2 fonts.**
One for headings, one for body. That's it. A third font is where brands start looking amateur.

**Don't go sterile.**
The warm cream background is non-negotiable. Pure white (#FFFFFF) feels clinical. You're a community, not a SaaS product.

**Don't chase trends.**
No glassmorphism, no bento grids, no AI-generated hero illustrations. Clean and warm ages well.

**Don't forget mobile.**
Your members will hit this site from the parking lot at the course. If it doesn't work beautifully on a phone, the desktop version doesn't matter.

## Color System

The palette pairs midnight green with community orange. This combo delivers confidence and warmth. Midnight green is timeless and connects to golf, while the orange provides a modern, approachable pop.

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Default page background. Never pure white. |
| Primary | Midnight Green | `#004C54` | Primary text, headings, dark surfaces. Timeless and grounded. |
| Accent | Community Orange | `#D4691C` | CTAs, links, highlights, data callouts. Your signature pop — use sparingly. |

### Supporting Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Surface | Deep Green | `#002B30` | Footer, dark sections, nav overlays. |
| Neutral | Stadium Silver | `#B8B5AF` | Secondary text, captions, borders, metadata. |
| Light Surface | Sand | `#EDE6DA` | Cards, callout boxes, alternating sections. Slightly darker than background. |

### Usage Rules
- **Community Orange is the only "loud" color.** Use it for buttons, links, key data points. If everything is orange, nothing is.
- **Midnight Green is the workhorse.** Headings, body text, dark surfaces, date blocks, stat bars.
- **Stadium Silver handles utility** — borders, captions, secondary text. It never draws attention.
- **Deep Green only for dark sections** — footer, overlays, inverted hero sections.
- **No additional colors.** If you need a status color (error, success), desaturate into the warm family.

### Palette in Context
- Hero section: Warm Cream background, Midnight Green headline, Community Orange CTA button.
- Event card: Sand card on Cream page, Midnight Green date block, Orange accent details, Silver metadata.
- Stats bar: Midnight Green background, Orange numbers, Cream labels.
- Footer: Deep Green background, Cream wordmark, Orange links.

## Typography

Two fonts. No exceptions.

**Headings: Outfit**
Geometric, modern, with slightly rounded terminals — friendly but not soft. Clean, contemporary feel. Available on Google Fonts.

**Body: DM Sans**
Clean, neutral, highly readable. Complements Outfit without competing. Available on Google Fonts.

### Type Scale
| Element | Size | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | 36–48px | 800 (ExtraBold) | Tight letter-spacing (–0.035em) |
| Section heading | 26–32px | 800 | letter-spacing –0.03em |
| Subhead | 18–22px | 600–700 | |
| Body text | 16px | 400 (Regular) | DM Sans, line-height 1.7 |
| Event card title | 15px | 700 (Bold) | Outfit |
| Card body / format | 14px | 400 | DM Sans |
| Labels / tags | 12–13px | 600 | Outfit, uppercase, letterspaced |
| Captions / meta | 13–14px | 400 | DM Sans, Stadium Silver color |

### Rules
- **Minimum readable size: 12px.** Nothing on the site should be smaller.
- **Line height:** 1.7 for body, 1.1–1.2 for headings.
- **Letter spacing:** tight on headings (–0.02 to –0.035em), normal or slightly wide on uppercase labels (0.04–0.12em).
- **Wordmark:** "CH TOURNAMENTS" set in Outfit ExtraBold, all caps, 0.04em letter-spacing.

## Logo

A clean, modern mark representing the tournament community. The design should feel confident and accessible.

### Design Principles
- Flat, clean execution — no gradients, no bevel, no 3D shadows
- Thick strokes for scalability at small sizes (favicon, ball marker, hat)
- Midnight Green primary, Community Orange accent

### Required Versions
1. **Full color** — Midnight Green + Orange on Cream background
2. **Reversed** — Cream mark on Midnight Green background
3. **Single-color green** — All Midnight Green on Cream
4. **Single-color cream** — All Cream on Midnight Green
5. **Favicon** — Simplified mark at 32×32px

### Wordmark Lockup
"CH TOURNAMENTS" in Outfit ExtraBold, all caps. Can be used with or without a mark.

## Photography & Imagery Style

- **Candid over posed.** Action shots, post-round beers, leaderboard drama, group photos where someone is mid-laugh.
- **Slightly warm edit.** Lean warm and slightly desaturated — don't oversaturate greens.
- **People over courses.** Your differentiator is the community, not the venues. Lead with faces.
- **No stock.** Ever. If you don't have a photo for a section, leave it as clean type on cream.

---

## Voice & Tone

Your copy should sound like the group text after a great round — not a golf resort brochure.

- **Confident, not try-hard.** You know how to run events. You don't need to sell how fun you are; show it.
- **Informal but not sloppy.** Contractions fine. Emojis sparingly. Typos aren't "voice."
- **Direct and inclusive.** "All skill levels" isn't a disclaimer, it's a value.
- **Short.** If a sentence can be cut, cut it. If a section can be a sentence, make it one.

## Social Media Templates

Keep these dead simple and consistent. Build 4–5 Canva/Figma templates and reuse forever.

- **Event announcement:** Cream background, Midnight Green headline, Community Orange date callout, mark bottom corner.
- **Recap post:** Full-bleed candid photo, wordmark overlaid in cream (bottom corner), no text overlay — story lives in the caption.
- **Leaderboard / results:** Sand background, clean table layout, Orange highlight on winner, Green text.
- **Story template:** Thin Orange border frame + mark, reusable for any content.

## Website Sections (Landing Page Spec)

1. **Nav** — Sticky, frosted-glass cream background. "CH TOURNAMENTS" wordmark left, minimal nav links right.
2. **Hero** — Bold headline, supporting copy, two CTAs (Browse Tournaments + Join), stats strip in Midnight Green (tournaments, members, rounds, winners).
3. **About** — What CH Tournaments is, core values (competition, community, inclusivity, transparency).
4. **Events** — Filterable schedule (Active / Upcoming / History) using real season data. Each card shows date block, event name, format, player count.
5. **How It Works** — Explanation of tournament formats and scoring.
6. **Leaderboards & Results** — Live tournament standings and final results with payouts.
7. **Player Profiles** — Handicap tracking, stats, round history, upcoming events.
8. **Join** — Simple CTA: Sign up, set handicap, join tournaments.
9. **Footer** — Deep Green, cream wordmark, orange links, social links.
