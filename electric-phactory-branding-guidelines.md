# The Electric Phactory — Branding Guidelines

*Updated: Midnight Green Edition. A reference doc for the EP's visual identity. Warm, confident, unmistakably Philly — not country club, not corporate, not generic startup.*

---

## The "Don't" List

These are the traps golf communities fall into. Avoid all of them.

**Don't look like a country club.**
No crests, no coat of arms, no crossed-clubs logos, no script/cursive typefaces, no dark green and gold "traditional" palettes. That's the thing you're the antidote to.

**Don't use cliché golf imagery.**
No stock photos of a guy in a tucked-in polo mid-backswing. No aerial drone shots of fairways as hero banners. If you use photography, it should be *your people at your events* — candid, real, slightly chaotic. The NLU/RGC aesthetic, not Golf Digest.

**Don't over-design.**
Tons of whitespace. Minimal navigation. No gradients layered on gradients. Restraint signals confidence.

**Don't use more than 2 fonts.**
One for headings, one for body. That's it. A third font is where brands start looking amateur.

**Don't go sterile.**
The warm cream background is non-negotiable. Pure white (#FFFFFF) feels clinical. You're a community, not a SaaS product.

**Don't chase trends.**
No glassmorphism, no bento grids, no AI-generated hero illustrations. Clean and warm ages well.

**Don't forget mobile.**
Your members will hit this site from the parking lot at the course. If it doesn't work beautifully on a phone, the desktop version doesn't matter.

**Don't over-brand NLU.**
You're *a* Roost, but you're building *the* Electric Phactory. Lead with your identity. NLU affiliation is context, not headline.

## Color System

The palette pairs Eagles midnight green with Phactory orange. This combo doesn't exist in any pro sports palette — it's uniquely EP. Midnight green is the most recognizable color in Philadelphia, and the golf/green connection is a freebie you don't need to explain.

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Warm Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Default page background. Never pure white. |
| Primary | Midnight Green | `#004C54` | Primary text, headings, dark surfaces. Eagles DNA meets fairway green. |
| Accent | Phactory Orange | `#D4691C` | CTAs, links, highlights, data callouts. Your signature pop — use sparingly. |

### Supporting Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Surface | Deep Green | `#002B30` | Footer, dark sections, nav overlays. After-hours at the Linc. |
| Neutral | Stadium Silver | `#B8B5AF` | Secondary text, captions, borders, metadata. Linc · CBP · WFC concrete. |
| Light Surface | Sand | `#EDE6DA` | Cards, callout boxes, alternating sections. Slightly darker than background. |

### Usage Rules
- **Phactory Orange is the only "loud" color.** Use it for buttons, links, key data points. If everything is orange, nothing is.
- **Midnight Green is the workhorse.** Headings, body text, dark surfaces, date blocks, stat bars.
- **Stadium Silver handles utility** — borders, captions, secondary text. It never draws attention.
- **Deep Green only for dark sections** — footer, overlays, inverted hero sections.
- **No additional colors.** If you need a status color (error, success), desaturate into the warm family.

### Palette in Context
- Hero section: Warm Cream background, Midnight Green headline, "Just golf." in Phactory Orange, Orange CTA button.
- Event card: Sand card on Cream page, Midnight Green date block, Orange month label, Silver course name.
- Stats bar: Midnight Green background, Orange numbers, Cream labels.
- Footer: Deep Green background, Cream wordmark, Orange links.

### Color Combos for Merch & Lockups
- **Primary:** Orange on Midnight Green (hat embroidery, ball marker, social avatar)
- **Secondary:** Midnight Green on Cream (clean summer feel, light merch)
- **Inverted:** Cream on Midnight Green (reversed logo, dark-mode, profile pic)
- **Bold:** Cream on Orange (used sparingly — poster callouts, special events)

## Typography

Two fonts. No exceptions.

**Headings: Outfit**
Geometric, modern, with slightly rounded terminals — friendly but not soft. Fits the "Electric" / industrial Philly energy. Available on Google Fonts.

**Body: DM Sans**
Clean, neutral, highly readable. Complements Outfit without competing. Available on Google Fonts.

### Type Scale
| Element | Size | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | 36–48px | 800 (ExtraBold) | Tight letter-spacing (–0.035em) |
| Section heading | 26–32px | 800 | letter-spacing –0.03em |
| Subhead | 18–22px | 600–700 | |
| Body text | 16px | 400 (Regular) | DM Sans, line-height 1.7 |
| Event card title | 15px | 700 (Bold) | Outfit |
| Card body / format | 14px | 400 | DM Sans |
| Labels / tags | 12–13px | 600 | Outfit, uppercase, letterspaced |
| Captions / meta | 13–14px | 400 | DM Sans, Stadium Silver color |

### Rules
- **Minimum readable size: 12px.** Nothing on the site should be smaller.
- **Line height:** 1.7 for body, 1.1–1.2 for headings.
- **Letter spacing:** tight on headings (–0.02 to –0.035em), normal or slightly wide on uppercase labels (0.04–0.12em).
- **Wordmark:** "ELECTRIC PHACTORY" set in Outfit ExtraBold, all caps, 0.04em letter-spacing.

## Logo

The mark is a stylized **P with a lightning bolt** integrated into the descender.

### Current Direction
- **P in Midnight Green (#004C54), bolt in Phactory Orange (#D4691C)**
- Flat, clean execution — no gradients, no bevel, no 3D shadows
- Thick strokes for scalability at small sizes (favicon, ball marker, hat)

### Required Versions
1. **Full color** — Midnight Green P + Orange bolt on Cream background
2. **Reversed** — Cream mark on Midnight Green background
3. **Single-color green** — All Midnight Green on Cream
4. **Single-color cream** — All Cream on Midnight Green
5. **Favicon** — Simplified mark at 32×32px

### Wordmark Lockup
"ELECTRIC PHACTORY" in Outfit ExtraBold, all caps, with the P mark to the left. Can be used with or without the mark.

### Don'ts
- Don't use the old red/navy colorway
- Don't add gradients or dimensional effects
- Don't place on busy photographic backgrounds
- Don't stretch, rotate, or alter proportions

## Photography & Imagery Style

- **Candid over posed.** Action shots, post-round beers, leaderboard drama, group photos where someone is mid-laugh.
- **Slightly warm edit.** Lean warm and slightly desaturated — don't oversaturate greens.
- **People over courses.** Your differentiator is the community, not the venues. Lead with faces.
- **No stock.** Ever. If you don't have a photo for a section, leave it as clean type on cream.

## Voice & Tone

Your copy should sound like the group text after a great round — not a golf resort brochure.

- **Confident, not try-hard.** You won the first Roost Club Championship and three Northeast Regionals. You don't need to sell how fun you are; show it.
- **Informal but not sloppy.** Contractions fine. Emojis sparingly. Typos aren't "voice."
- **Philly energy.** Direct, a little irreverent, zero pretension. Think Wawa, not Wegmans.
- **Inclusive by default.** "All skill levels" isn't a disclaimer, it's a value.
- **Short.** If a sentence can be cut, cut it. If a section can be a sentence, make it one.
- **"Meaningless but structured competition."** This phrase from the NLU ethos is the EP's north star. Use it.

## Social Media Templates

Keep these dead simple and consistent. Build 4–5 Canva/Figma templates and reuse forever.

- **Event announcement:** Cream background, Midnight Green headline, Phactory Orange date callout, EP mark bottom corner.
- **Recap post:** Full-bleed candid photo, EP wordmark overlaid in cream (bottom corner), no text overlay — story lives in the caption.
- **Leaderboard / results:** Sand background, clean table layout, Orange highlight on winner, Green text.
- **Story template:** Thin Orange border frame + EP mark, reusable for any content.
